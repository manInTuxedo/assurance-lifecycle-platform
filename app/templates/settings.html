Where the platform stands

Reset the platform

For starting a test run from nothing. Only an administrator sees this, and every reset asks you to type the word first. User accounts are never deleted — a reset must not lock you out of your own platform.

Assessment history

Deletes every finding, exception and uploaded assessment. The asset inventory, the SLA policy and the accounts stay, so the next upload lands on assets that are already known.

History and inventory

The above, plus the asset register. The inventory file on disk is loaded again straight after, so you are back to assets with no findings.

Factory reset

Everything: findings, exceptions, assessments, assets, the SLA policy and the change log. The default policy is seeded again and the inventory is reloaded. Accounts are kept.
